14 Day Return Policy

Simple & customer-friendly return process

We want every customer to be fully satisfied with their purchase. If you are not happy with your order, you may request a return within 14 days of receiving the product.

Return Conditions

  • Product must be unused and in original condition.
  • Original packaging should be included.
  • Damaged or used items may not qualify for return.
  • Return request must be made within 14 days.

Refund Process

After receiving and inspecting the returned item, the refund will be processed within a few working days.

Exchange Option

Customers can also request product exchange depending on stock availability.
